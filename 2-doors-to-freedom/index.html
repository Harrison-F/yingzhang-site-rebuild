BACKGROUND

The Human Rights Foundation's Art in Protest program is a response to the repression of creative dissent. The program elevates artists whose work advances democracy and human rights, recognizing art's power to challenge authoritarianism and create space for dialogue.

CHALLENGE

The challenge was to create an experience that honored the risks artists take in defending freedom of expression, while showcasing their work and creating a platform for dialogue and engagement as part of Art Basel Miami Beach.

SOLUTION

Doors to Freedom is a series of sculptures that reimagines the architecture of imprisonment as a framework for visibility and resistance. Structures inspired by spaces of confinement are transformed into surfaces that elevate the artists’ work, turning symbols of repression into spaces for connection and conversation.
