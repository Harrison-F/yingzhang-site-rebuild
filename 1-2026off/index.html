FREEDOM FORUM

BACKGROUND

Dismantling Dictatorship was the theme for the 2026 Oslo Freedom Forum, a three-day human rights conference in Oslo bringing together more than 1,500 activists, journalists, technologists, artists, and thinkers from around the world.

CHALLENGE

The theme framed authoritarianism as something constructed rather than inevitable: a system of power that can be understood, challenged, and dismantled. The challenge was to give visual form to the complex and abstract idea of dismantling authoritarian power, representing a regime as a structure that can be methodically taken apart.

SOLUTION

The visual language treats authoritarianism like a machine: assembled from individual parts, and therefore capable of being disassembled. breaking part letterforms, pulling apart words and motion that is staccato and mechanical, whilst the color palette draws from utility and workwear, Together, these elements create a visual identity that makes dismantling visible through reduction, and deconstruction. The system reflects the Forum’s central proposition: authoritarianism is not inevitable. It is built, and what is built can be taken apart, separated, and dismantled until nothing remains.
