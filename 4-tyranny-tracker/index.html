TYRANNY TRACKER

TYRANNY TRACKER

BACKGROUND

With approximately 75% of the world living under authoritarian rule, Tyranny Tracker is a democracy index documenting how authoritarian regimes consolidate power, and where democratic resilience persists. The project was created to make complex political research more accessible and help journalists, policymakers, activists, and the public better understand the global state of freedom.

CHALLENGE

The digital experience needed to communicate complex political analysis immediately and legibly to a broad audience without sacrificing nuance, while remaining rigorous enough for journalists, policymakers, and human rights organizations.

SOLUTION

We created a visual language and interactive digital experience that was easy to navigate, and clearly represented political systems, data, and patterns of authoritarian consolidation. The system was designed to work  across digital and physical environments, allowing the identity to move from the microsite into the launch experience at One World Trade Center. At the launch, the visual language became an immersive expression of the project itself, surrounding an audience of people working directly on the front lines of the fight for freedom.
