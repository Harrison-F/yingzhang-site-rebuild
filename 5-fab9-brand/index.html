FAB9 BRAND

FAB9 BRAND

FAB9 BRAND

BACKGROUND

FAB9 is a makerspace startup founded with the mission of democratizing access to making. Through a monthly subscription, FAB9 provides members with access to high-end prototyping equipment, creating a new kind of shared workspace that fosters community, creativity, and meaningful connections through making.

CHALLENGE

As a startup, FAB9 needed a brand built from the ground up, one that represented its mission, vision, and values. It was essential that the identity appealed to both experienced and new makers alike, the duality of capturing the technological aspect of the product whilst being approachable and inviting.

SOLUTION

We created a brand that is dynamic, scalable and democratic. The visual identity is versatile, flexible and adaptable, capturing the diversity of FAB9 members, the community and their creations.
