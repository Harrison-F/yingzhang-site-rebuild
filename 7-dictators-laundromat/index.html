DICTATOR'S LAUNDROMAT

BACKGROUND

BACKGROUND

Kleptocracy operates largely out of public view. Its mechanisms — shell companies, lobbying networks, financial intermediaries and reputation laundering are abstract, technical and difficult to communicate. The Dictator's Laundromat is an immersive installation created for the 2025 SXSW Festival, that exposes the hidden systems allowing authoritarian leaders to conceal, move, and legitimize stolen wealth

CHALLENGE

CHALLENGE

The challenge was to transform a complex geopolitical issue into an experience that is immediate, accessible, and engaging in a crowded festival environment.

SOLUTION

SOLUTION

Inspired by suburban laundromats, the activation uses an almost synthetic bubblegum palette to create tension between a sense of playfulness and the serious subject matter. Rather than presenting information as policy or advocacy, the installation translates complex financial networks into visual stories that reward exploration. The installation attracted record attendance and expanded visibility for the Combating Kleptocracy program, demonstrating the power of a creative concept to bring complex political issues to life.
